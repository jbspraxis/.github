## Order, made visible.

JBS Praxis is a consulting and engineering firm for Africa’s institutions. We design how organisations run, then build the systems that answer for them, in English and French.

We don’t sell software licences. We design how an area of your organisation should work, then build the system that runs it, connected to the tools you keep.

### The Praxis Method

- **See:** We sit with the people who do the work and map how one area really runs: who does what, where the numbers live, where time and money leak.
- **Design:** We design how it should run: the process, the controls, who approves what, and the few numbers that matter.
- **Build:** We build and configure the system around that design, connect the tools you keep, and move your data across.
- **Hold:** We stay until it holds: we train your people, watch the first cycles, fix what the real world reveals, then hand over the keys.

You own the code and the data. No per-user licences. English and French from day one.

### How we build

- **Roles and permissions** on every screen and action, so people see what their job needs and nothing more.
- **A record of everything**: sensitive actions are written to an audit log.
- **Separation of duties**: whoever prepares a payment or a payroll run can’t approve it.
- **Private by default**: documents and receipts open only through signed, permissioned links.
- Processes **aligned with ISO 27001**, under Nigeria’s Data Protection Act and Cameroon’s data protection law.
